Data Entry

Enter field readings; pressure and pore-water elevation are calculated from the Excel calibration factors.

Calibration factors (kPa)—
Calculated water pressure— m H₂O
Calculated pore-water elevation— mRL
Enter the date, frequency, and temperature to calculate this reading.

Excel calculation: Pressure (m H₂O) = (A × Frequency² + B × Frequency + C) × 0.10197; elevation (mRL) = pressure + sensor elevation. Temperature is saved but is not used in this formula.
